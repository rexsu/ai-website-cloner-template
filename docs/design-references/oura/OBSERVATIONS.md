## Typography

### Style:
- **Primary Font**: Serif typeface (appears to be a refined serif like a modernized Playfair or similar)
  - Used for main headlines: "Wear your best life", "Understand your body. Own your health."
  - Large sizes: 60-80px range for heroes
  - Font weight: Light to medium (300-500)
  - Letter spacing: Normal to slightly loose
  
- **Secondary Font**: Clean sans-serif (likely Inter, Helvetica, or similar)
  - Used for body text, buttons, navigation
  - Body text: ~16-18px
  - Nav links: ~14-16px

### Hierarchy:
- H1: Large serif, light weight
- H2/H3: Smaller serif or medium weight sans
- Body: Sans-serif, regular weight
- CTAs: Sans-serif, medium/semibold

## Overall Design Philosophy

### Brand Feel:
- **Premium**: High-quality photography, generous whitespace, refined typography
- **Wellness-focused**: Calming colors (peachy beige, soft backgrounds)
- **Modern minimalism**: Clean layouts, sans clutter
- **Human-centered**: Real people in imagery, testimonials, relatable scenarios
- **Tech-forward**: Sleek product photography, app showcase, dark UI accents
- **Trustworthy**: Press mentions, statistics, user testimonials
- **Accessible**: Clear hierarchy, good contrast, readable fonts

### Key Patterns:
- **Serif headlines + Sans body**: Classic premium pairing
- **Rounded pill buttons**: Friendly, modern
- **Dark + Light sections**: Creates rhythm and emphasis
- **Photography-led**: Minimal graphic elements, photos tell the story
- **Blue primary CTA**: High contrast, action-oriented
- **Carousel/scroll patterns**: Accommodates rich content without overwhelming
- **Layered mockups**: Adds depth and interest to app/product showcases
